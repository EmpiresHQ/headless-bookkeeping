# Delivery channel as a first-class intake-policy axis (Ingest profiles)

Email intake adds two new ways a **Document** enters the kernel: a **Mailbox connector** (pull — we hold read-only access to the Organization owner's *own* mailbox and harvest invoice attachments on a schedule) and the email **Channel adapter** (push — a counterparty/approver emails *our* bot address, ADR-0025). These join the existing arrival paths (`upload`, `telegram`, `ios_sync`, `drive`). The pull path is the new pressure: it is an **ambient firehose** — most messages are not invoices, some are personal, some are invoices addressed to other parties — whereas every other path is a **deliberate send** (the operator or a counterparty consciously handed us this document). This ADR fixes how the *arrival path* governs intake disposition. It builds on ADR-0024 (two-pass OCR ingestion) and ADR-0025 (channel-adapter seam); it does not touch the kernel, **Rules**, or **Policy** (the voucher risk gate is unchanged).

## The cut

- **The delivery channel is a first-class axis of intake policy, not just provenance.** A channel carries an **intent prior**: deliberate-push channels (`upload`, `telegram`, `ios_sync`, `email_push`) imply high intent → permissive handling; the ambient-pull channel (`email_sync`) implies low intent → conservative, content-gated handling. The axis is *intent*, not *medium* — `email_push` (counterparty → our bot) and `email_sync` (we harvest the owner's mailbox) share the wire format but sit on **opposite** sides.

- **Each channel resolves an `Ingest profile`** — a small, **fixed** set of operator-configurable knobs (v1): accept a document with no named recipient (a receipt) y/n; accept photo/image attachments vs PDFs only; dispose of a non-invoice / wrong-recipient document as a silent **`discarded`** vs a `needs_triage` finding. Defaults differ by channel's intent prior (deliberate → permissive + `needs_triage`; ambient → strict + `discarded`); overridable **per-company**, and optionally **per connector instance** (the owner's personal Gmail strict, a dedicated `invoices@` mailbox lenient). It is **not** a rules engine.

- **A new terminal Document state `discarded`** is the quiet-drop counterpart to `needs_triage`: retained and retrievable for audit and anti-re-harvest, but raising no finding and never nagging the operator. Which terminal a non-match lands in is the channel's `Ingest profile` decision.

- **Disposition is a source-aware layer over the existing pipeline, not a fork of it.** A harvested document still flows `upload() → status='pending' → kick() → IntakeQueueWorker → IntakeWorkflowService.process()` (the serialized intake queue is reused verbatim — the connector only *fetches from IMAP*; it never runs OCR itself). The `Ingest profile` is applied as a thin layer **over** the `IntakeWorkflowResult`, keyed on the document's delivery channel — it does not reach into Pass-2 or the country plugin.

## Why

Chosen over (a) **one global disposition policy** — which cannot satisfy both paths at once: routing every ambiguous document to `needs_triage` would bury the operator under firehose noise from `email_sync` (personal mail, newsletters, others' invoices), while silently dropping ambiguous documents would erase the legitimate photo-receipts that arrive deliberately over Telegram/iOS. The disposition *has* to be a function of how the document arrived. And over (b) **a general per-channel rules engine** — over-engineered for a single-tenant bookkeeping system; a bounded knob set covers the real cases (YAGNI on a DSL), and can grow if a concrete need appears. Keying the profile on the **intent prior** rather than the literal medium is what lets `email_push` reuse the Telegram-like permissive defaults while `email_sync`, same medium, runs strict.

## Consequences

- New domain terms (CONTEXT.md): **Delivery channel**, **Ingest profile**, **Discarded (Document)**.
- New config: per-channel `Ingest profile` knobs (accept-without-recipient, accept-photos, non-match-disposition), with per-company and per-connector overrides — surfaced via the admin settings HTTP API + operator SPA (ADR-0028).
- New `document.status` terminal value `discarded`, plus a retrievable "discarded" view in the SPA.
- `IntakeWorkflowService` routing becomes **channel-aware** at the disposition layer — a coordination seam with the concurrent **claimant-reimbursement** work (ADR-0036). Pass-2 emits a single 3-valued `recipient_match` (`ours | other_party | none`, extraction owned by email-sync) from which claimant's `company_addressed_receipt` boolean is derived; the *disposition* of a mismatch is decided by the `Ingest profile` over the result. Disposition precedence is `claimant → needs_triage` **>** Ingest-profile **>** Policy. Kept as distinct layers (extraction vs disposition) so the two efforts do not collide. See the email-intake spec for the full handoff.
- The **Mailbox connector** (pull) is ingestion-only and read-only (cross-ref ADR-0025: it resolves no **Principal** from the sender and carries no converse/approval traffic). Its credential storage and OAuth/IMAP transport are a separate concern (own spec).
- The same invoice arriving on two channels still collapses to one **Document** (the dedup anchor); the `Ingest profile` of whichever channel admitted it governs its disposition.
